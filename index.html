About me

I’m Haoran Yu, a graduate student at Columbia University in the City of New York. I’m pursuing a Master of Arts in Statistics through its Graduate School of Arts and Sciences (GSAS).

Before Columbia, I studied mathematics at the University of Toronto, with minors in statistics and economics. I graduated with an Honours BSc in October 2025.

At Columbia, I study how to draw conclusions from data and how to describe quantities that change randomly over time. I’m especially interested in how these ideas apply to financial markets.

This project presents a loan‐default prediction model on LendingClub’s 2007–2010 data that, by rebalancing the training set and using a Random Forest, achieves an AUC of 0.67 and captures 22% of actual defaults.

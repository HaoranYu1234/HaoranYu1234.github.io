About Me

My name is Haoran Yu. I graduated from the University of Toronto in October 2025 with an Honours Bachelor of Science in Mathematics major, and minors in Statistics and Economics. My academic training spans mathematics, probability and statistics, data analysis, machine learning, economics, and finance, providing a rigorous quantitative foundation. I apply these skills to the development of analytical insights and data-driven models for real-world problems, with a strong interest in continual learning and refinements.

Machine Learning Classification And Loan Default Prediction

This project presents a loan‐default prediction model on LendingClub’s 2007–2010 data that, by rebalancing the training set and using a Random Forest, achieves an AUC of 0.67 and captures 22% of actual defaults.

Key Skills: EDA, feature engineering, data visualization, and machine learning classification with scikit-learn.
